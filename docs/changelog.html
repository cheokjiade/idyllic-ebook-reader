1.1 — released 14 September 2026

Faster with a large library, and series get their own look. Hiding, unhiding or deleting many books at once now shows a running count instead of appearing frozen, the library tools that take a while show progress and some can be cancelled, and a series appears as a stack of covers you can favourite, open and organise.

Reading

  • The reader follows the app's Theme setting (Settings › Appearance) by default, and plain-text and RTF books now honour it too.
  • In continuous scroll, the bookmark button adds a bookmark where you are; the icon stays filled while that bookmark's screen is in view, and tapping it again removes it.
  • The search highlight in an EPUB sits exactly on the matched word.
  • A book removed while you are reading another one no longer leaves a ghost entry in the library.

Comics

  • The Navigation grid shows a thumbnail for every page, and the thumbnails are kept, so reopening a comic shows the full grid straight away.
  • Pre-process Upscaling runs without hitching the app, shows a progress bar in Book Detail, and its Cancel item shows the percentage done.

Listen

  • Play always speaks again after rapid pause, play and skip presses.
  • Previous sentence at the start of a chapter now reads the last sentence of the previous chapter, rather than jumping a whole chapter back.

Your library

  • A series looks like its layout: a stack of covers in Detailed, Compact and Grid, a count pill in Minimalist, and a segmented bar showing which volumes are read.
  • A series stack says where the rest of its volumes are — "+2 in Fantasy" — and a lone volume whose series lives elsewhere shows as a stack you can open.
  • Favourite a series: favourites sort first with a star, in the library and on the All Series screen, and appear under the Favorites filter.
  • Long-press a series stack for View Series, Select All Volumes, favourite and Series Settings.
  • Group by Series works in the Table and Magazine layouts too, and now lives in the filter sheet under Layout. The menu item is now "All Series".
  • Entering a series name that differs from an existing one only by case or spacing offers to join that series instead of creating a twin, and an imported book joins an existing spelling automatically. Joining merges the two series' settings, and a favourite on either survives.
  • Sort by Series puts books without a series last, and a series split across folders says which folders on its screen.
  • The Table layout gives the Title column the room Progress and Size were wasting, and in Magazine a folder row just above the hero card opens the folder.
  • Changing the sort keeps your place, applying a filter starts at the top, and switching to a more compact layout keeps the same books on screen. Switching layout right after launch lands on the stack containing your book.
  • On iPad, the library's sheets — Statistics, Reading History, Clean Duplicates, tags, the metadata editor, the series screens and Settings — no longer change the folder column behind them.
  • The User Guide documents series: Group by Series, stacks, the series screen and All Series.

Library tools

  • Deleting many selected books, and hiding or unhiding many at once, show an "Updating Library…" overlay with a running count instead of appearing frozen, and the library refreshes once at the end instead of after every book. Hiding a folder of books is much faster.
  • Unhide and then Hide can be used back to back in the same folder.
  • Re-extract All Metadata can be cancelled, no longer stalls on large files, and refreshes the library once when done.
  • Clean Duplicates and Repair Library scan without freezing the screen, and their spinners now appear. The duplicate cleanup's hidden-status moves run as one batch with progress.

Hidden books and sync

  • Hidden books are better protected when you leave the app or leave hidden mode, on iPhone and iPad alike.
  • Turning "Sync hidden books to iCloud" on or off shows progress and holds the settings sheet until it finishes; "Permanently Delete Hidden Books" and "Sync hidden reading stats to iCloud" show a spinner while they run.

Also

  • Two more bundled titles: Aesop's Fables (trans. George Fyler Townsend) and Edward Lear's Nonsense Songs, taking the starter library from five to seven.
  • The About screen shows the app's real name and version, and scrolls.

1.0 — released 4 September 2026

The first release. Idyllic is an offline-first reader for ebooks and comics: it opens files you already own, keeps them on your device, and asks for no account. There are no ads and no tracking.

Reading

  • Opens EPUB, PDF, TXT and RTF books.
  • Choose your typeface, text size, line spacing and screen brightness, with light, sepia and dark themes, or set your own colours.
  • Read by turning pages, with a page-flip animation, or as one continuous scroll.
  • Fixed-layout EPUBs — illustrated and picture books — render as designed, with zoom and facing-page spreads.
  • Vertical writing for Chinese, Japanese and Korean books, and optional pinyin above Chinese text.
  • Bookmarks, highlights and full-text search inside a book.

Comics

  • Opens CBZ, CBR and CB7 archives.
  • Read page by page, or as a continuous webtoon scroll.
  • Pinch to zoom in both modes, with right-to-left page order for manga.
  • Borders around scanned pages can be trimmed automatically.

Listen

  • Any book can be read aloud using the voices already on your device — no network, and nothing leaves the phone.
  • Playback continues when you leave the app, with title, chapter and cover art on the Lock Screen and in Control Center.
  • Choose a different voice per language, and follow along with the sentence highlighted as it is spoken.

Your library

  • Organise with folders, tags and favourites, and search across titles, authors, series, tags and publishers.
  • Series are grouped together, with a prompt for the next volume when you finish one.
  • Reading statistics show what you have read and how much time you have spent.

Getting books in

  • Import from the Files app, or send them from a computer over Wi-Fi by opening a page in your browser — no cable and no account.
  • Connect to your own OPDS catalogue or WebDAV server.

Privacy

  • Books can be hidden behind Face ID or Touch ID. Hidden books are stored separately and stay on this device unless you turn on "Sync hidden books to iCloud" yourself, which is off by default.
  • An optional privacy screen covers the app in the app switcher.

Sync

  • Optional iCloud sync keeps your library, reading positions and preferences together across your devices, using your own private iCloud storage.

Also

  • Translated into 26 languages.
  • Ships with five public-domain titles so there is something to read straight away — Alice's Adventures in Wonderland, The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, 紅樓夢, Grimms' Fairy Tales, and a collection of Japanese woodblock prints from The Met's open access collection.
